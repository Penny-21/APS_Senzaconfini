Our Projects

“Building bridges across Europe, one project at a time.”

🌍 What We Do: European projects that build connections, impact, and change

Senza Confini APS is not just a youth association: we are a permanent laboratory for European project design, a reference point for those who want to turn ideas into real impact. In recent years, we have developed, implemented, and supported innovative projects in Erasmus+, CERV, ESC, Creative Europe, Horizon Europe, LIFE, Interreg NEXT MED, and Virtual Exchange programmes.

Our strength lies in two things: we deeply understand the local context and its needs, and at the same time, we speak the language of Europe. We handle both project design and management, offering professional, reliable, and customized consulting services to third-sector organizations, public administrations, schools, and informal groups.

🎯 The projects we carry out

We create tailor-made paths for young people, educators, local communities, and professionals, always with an inclusive, innovative, and sustainable approach.

📌 Here are some examples of projects we’ve carried out:

  • Con i Bambini 2020
    2020-EDU-02341 With the Children _ Educating Communities: Project “UMBERTIAMO: EDUCATIONAL COMMUNITY UMBERTO I” — social inclusion, active citizenship, community cooperation, creation of an educational community in the Umbertino district of La Spezia
  • Erasmus+ KA2 2025
    KA210-YOU “Tech QUEENS - AI”: Young people from Italy and Luxembourg engaged in an AI literacy training journey through local environmental education activities and an international youth policy conference.
  • Erasmus+ KA2 2024–2025
    Tech Queens – g(AI)rl power: The official Tech Queens platform, an Erasmus+ KA210-YOU project run with our Luxembourg partner ALA and co-funded by the European Union. It empowers Gen Z women to lead in AI and technology through interactive courses, community support, and international events, building digital literacy and reducing the gender gap in AI.
    👉 Visit the Tech Queens platform
  • Erasmus+ Youth Exchange 2023–2024
    KA152-YOU-BA820889 & KA152-YOU-219A1096: Youth exchanges in La Spezia and across Europe to promote active citizenship, intercultural dialogue, and sustainability, involving 60+ young participants in creative, sporting, and participatory activities.
  • Social Enterprise €1.3M
    “Comunità Educanti Umberto I” (Con i Bambini): A €1.3 million project that revolutionized education in a city’s peripheral area, thanks to a network between schools, families, associations, and institutions.
    👉 See the project
  • Erasmus+ KA1 2024–2025
    KA1 Mobility Projects (2024–2025): We take part in international training projects for youth workers, promoting non-formal education on mental well-being, inclusion, and digital skills.
  • Erasmus+ KA2 2025
    KA210-YOU-CD3C7FA9 (Erasmus+ KA2 – 2025): Currently being launched, this project aims to build a Euro-Mediterranean network of young environmental activists and digital trainers.
